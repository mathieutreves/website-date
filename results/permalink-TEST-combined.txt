  htmldate leads on accuracy by 4.0 points and answers every page. pagedate is
  second, marginally ahead on precision, at a quarter of the per-page cost, and
  is the only tool here that will decline to answer — the 13 declines are 5.1
  points it cannot win back on a corpus where every page has a date.

  htmldate is invoked with `original_date=True`, which is the flag that asks for
  a publication date; every gold label here is one. It is the only tool measured
  with such a switch — every other exposes a publication date and nothing else.

  Note the pagedate row differs from permalink-pagedate-TEST.txt (230 exact,
  90.9%). That file is scripts/corpus/score.ts, which blanks the dated permalink
  only where the page declares it — <link rel="canonical"> and og:url. This
  harness blanks every date-shaped path in the raw HTML, for every tool alike,
  because a competitor with no holdOut mechanism would otherwise read the label
  out of an <a href>. It is the stricter measurement and the one to quote when
  comparing tools; score.ts is the one to quote for pagedate on its own.
